These are based on how hero works and this is mostly Sammie's project so she can trump them.

HOW WE WORK
- Every change goes: discuss, plan, ask to write, write.
- Only push or merge to main when told to but do not make a pull request.
- Plans are letters (Plan A, B...), steps are numbers, and questions are Q1, Q2...
- Small changes need only a short discussion. Take requests literally and don't add extras.
  If he asks for only a new look, keep the mechanics exactly the same.
- For art, try at least 6 versions, pick the best, and show screenshots.
- Check that abilities actually do what their names say, and measure them if unsure.
- Always check the relevant part of the code or necessary information before suggesting a change.

CO-OP FAIRNESS
- Lives are shared and never created. Someone joining takes lives from whoever has the most until everyone
  is within one life of each other. Someone leaving gives their lives to whoever has the fewest.
- Starting together doesn't give extra lives, because that would make co-op stronger than playing alone.
- Running out of lives costs you every power-up and boost from that run, and nobody else gets them.
  That's the price of being carried in co-op. You keep your candy.
- SECOND WIND wipes you the same way, then brings you back with 2 lives.

PROGRESSION
- Each player number (P1-P4) has its own candy, characters and upgrades.
- Candy goes to whoever catches it.
- Unlocks happen only in the menu between games, never during play.
- Characters cost 500 candy and upgrades cost 100, with no free upgrades. Characters can be bought in any order.
- Each character is a bit better at one thing, its SPECIAL, worth 2 boosts: Ghost fire rate, Cat move speed,
  Zombie damage, Skeleton shot speed, Jester longer safety (he's built around taking a hit and bouncing back).
  Its page in CHARACTERS names it (SPECIAL: ... +2); the join picker never does.
- SUGAR RUSH (cotton candy): locked until the PUMPKIN KING has been beaten once on the save (team-wide flag,
  /debug sugarrush on|off to test). Then a boss tosses one high into the air when it dies, two if 3-4 players
  are alive at that moment. It falls beside the candy stream, not through it: you choose which to go for. Each one caught adds +1 to the catcher's SPECIAL, up to 10 (so +12 in all), and it's lost with
  the power-ups and boosts on death. The HUD shows SUG 1 to SUG 10 on the prestige line, in its style, since
  it's more special than the power-ups. Build back up by surviving, not handouts.
- The treat bucket's POWER TREAT (5%) is one of the pumpkin's small boosts, never a character ability.
  The character's own boost is its "Special", so a bucket never says special.
- PRESTIGE: once a character and all its upgrades are bought, it can be relocked for +1 of every boost
  for that character, up to 10 times. Prices for it go up 10% of the base each time. Not your only
  character unless you have the candy to buy one back. Shows as PRESTIGE 1/10 on its page, PRE 1 in game.
- ON/OFF in the menu decides which upgrades can drop. You don't start a run with them.
- The pumpkin drops a catchable power-up, with no equip screen.
- After all your upgrades are collected, pumpkins give small boosts that stack slowly. That way late waves still matter.
- A boost collected during a run never beats the character ability it matches. It stacks on top of it,
  so the Black Cat with LUCKY COIN has the biggest shots. Prestige can go past that.

WAVES AND ENEMIES
- Everything is on a doubled scale (orbs do 2, bats and witches have 2 HP, vampires 12, spiders 6).
  Each stage beaten (the PUMPKIN KING, then the GRIM REAPER) adds 1 HP to every enemy after it.
- SPECTRAL SHOT and PUNCH THROUGH halve with each enemy they go through and stop under 1 damage, so they can't clear whole screens. There's no cap on how many they go through.
- Each enemy enters its own way: bats swarm in from the corners, witches swoop in from the sides firing,
  vampires come as dark red-eyed bats and transform.
- Spiders hang on threads and web the spot below a front enemy. Webs only stop player shots (piercing too),
  except that PUNCH THROUGH carries on through a web tile it breaks, halving like it does through an enemy.
  With its webs up a spider spits venom instead.
- Every enemy has its own chance to attack, about one attack per row of a kind at a time, with a cap.
- Attack cadence: a shared volley window, then a rest. The rests are the gaps. Nothing attacks until the
  formation has settled.
- Difficulty is one number, the wave's LEVEL, that drives every attack rate and shot speed. No caps anywhere:
  caps are never the right way, diminishing returns with a floor is (it stays getting harder).
  Within a stage the level follows the cadence: set 1 relaxed, set 2 medium, set 3 hard, set 4 harder, set 5
  find the gaps or get hit. The wave right after a boss is a breather (lower level, one row fewer). Each stage
  beaten adds a step: 0.5 * (1 - e^(-stages/2.5)) + 0.06 * stages. Sets 1-2 (into 3) must stay survivable
  enough that a team thinned by the last boss can build back up through surviving, not through handouts.
- Every attack has a tell in proportion to its punishment (see Game-Design-Document.md).
- Bosses: PRIMAL BAT, BLACK WIDOW, VAMPIRE LORD, WITCH QUEEN, PUMPKIN KING. A boss's minions show up in the
  waves just before it, as a warning, and only then (spiders replaced up to 6 only before the BLACK WIDOW,
  the extra vampire row only before the VAMPIRE LORD, the extra witch row only before the WITCH QUEEN).
  After the PUMPKIN KING comes STAGE 1 COMPLETE, then Sammie's HAUNTED MANSION (25 waves, bosses in order
  SPIDER BEAST < BAT CAT < PUPPET DOLL < THE TWINS < GRIM REAPER). After the GRIM REAPER comes STAGE 2 COMPLETE,
  and it starts over in the graveyard at wave 1's lineup with every enemy tougher (+1 HP per stage beaten).
- Mansion waves 1-4 ease in with a slight slow mix, not one kind per wave. Before a boss, half the enemies in
  each wave are its kind, as in the graveyard: spiders before the SPIDER BEAST, bats before BAT CAT (her bats
  recoloured as small PRIMAL BATs, not new art), dolls before the PUPPET DOLL, ghosts before the GRIM REAPER.
  THE TWINS' kind is Sammie's to say; until then those waves are the plain full room. The mummy and
  Frankenstein sprites stay unused: don't add them just because the art exists.
- Mansion HP: dolls and ghosts 4, spiders 3 (the grunts: a dropping spider can be shot out of your lane),
  bats 2, +1 per stage beaten.
- The GRIM REAPER's job is to test the whole stage: he borrows the other bosses' attacks and calls the room's
  kinds. A second form is a Sammie discussion.
- The wave counter counts 1-25 in each stage, starting again at WAVE 1 in the mansion and back in the graveyard.
  The real wave count keeps going underneath for difficulty.
- Every boss attack must leave a hole. Play test of stage 2 (two Zombies, a ROT CLOUD up the whole time):
  waves calm, PUPPET DOLL just right, BAT CAT far too fast (dove the instant it picked you, claws both ways),
  THE TWINS' bow walls had no lane. So: the cat crouches for half a second first, claws 45 px, a rest after
  landing, half-rate stream; the Twins' walls are four bows 36 px apart and their streams take turns; the
  SPIDER BEAST's web slows for 1 s and doesn't restart while you're slowed; sets 3-5 are a step harder.
- ROT CLOUD: 25% slow, each further cloud adds half the last (75, 63, 56, 53%): diminishing returns, no cap.
- Brought in from Sammie's branch: the Grim Reaper's skull-in-a-cloak look, the Spider Beast's pounce (circle on
  the floor, straight drop on its thread), the Bat Cat's straight drop with one dark scratch (the slide over is
  the tell; our rest after landing and half-rate stream stay). Her Pumpkin King face goes on the title's centre
  pumpkin only; in play the power-up pumpkin flips a coin between her face and ours. The WITCH QUEEN waits for her last attack's shots to pass the players
  (an aimed shot excepted, as long as it doesn't home) plus a short random breather before the next one.
  Only the PUMPKIN KING gets a ring of shots. The VAMPIRE LORD stays distant and commanding, so he doesn't
  dive at you himself; he sends bats instead.

MENUS AND CONTROLS
- The title screen has a proper QUIT, and Start/Esc gives you options.
- On a controller, B goes back and LB/RB switch tabs. Switching tabs keeps the page you're on.
- Start joins instantly with a small character picker that doesn't pause the game.
  It only pauses if you're already playing. The picker has a "?" for random.
- Each slot has a FAVOURITE character, picked from a drop-down (icon and name) in PLAYER SETUP. CHARACTERS is
  for buying and upgrades only (no PLAY AS). PLAY asks whoever pressed it which character, defaulting to their
  favourite; a pick there or when joining is for that game only and never changes the favourite.
- In the upgrade pop-up, the name is biggest, the description goes right under it,
  and the player number is played down.
- The Jester isn't a grey silhouette on the title screen while locked; once bought he pops out of the right-hand
  pumpkin as a jack-in-the-box. The lid is literally the pumpkin's own cut top (same pixels), so it's the right
  size; the cut's back rim curves up behind him in pale flesh and the top of his collar just shows over the front.
- Settings: full screen and windowed are separate from window size. Window sizes are clean multiples
  of the game size, and real 16:9 can come later.
- Pause menu: "In game pause menu should give you access to the Characters screen so you can spend candy to
  unlock characters and abilities without going back to the main menu but it should not let you choose to set
  them as off or on from in game, if you buy one it defaults as on." PRESTIGE also waits for the main menu
  (relocking a character mid-run is a mess). "it should also have a power ups menu that shows each player in
  and what their power ups are", "only ones they have not any they dont have": icon, P number, abilities with
  code and name, boosts, PRE / SUG / the girl's rolls.
- "If you get a sugar rush but you're at max sugar rush it should say 500 points also the max pumpkin point bonus
  should be increased to 500", "the bottom corner of the title screen should have a version number like V. xxx"
  (V. 0.1 to start, one constant).
- Stage 1 tells (Hero): "Vampire Eyes could blink black 3 times before firing / Witch eyes could blink red once
  before firing / Bats could fly upward about 12 pixels before diving / Spider eyes blink green once before
  shooting venom shot, no extra tell for web / no new boss tells yet". Written: half a second, a quarter, a
  third of a second, a quarter.
- Debug console for testing: ~ opens a command line along the bottom, Enter runs it, ~ or Esc closes it.
  The game pauses while it's open and every key goes to it. Commands start with "/".
    /debug stage 1 or /debug stage 2   start that stage at wave 1, with the HP boost it would have
    /debug gc p3 500                   give that player slot (p1-p4) that much candy
    /debug sugarrush [on|off]          say whether SUGAR RUSH is LOCKED or UNLOCKED, or set it
    /debug littlegirl                  the next RANDOM roll is the LITTLE GIRL

                                       her again (p1 if no player is given)

    /debug <name> [p1-p4]              hold that ability or SHOP upgrade this run (name as CHARACTERS shows it,

    /debug prestige +N|-N <character> [p1-p4]   move the slot's prestige for that character, 0 to 10

CHARACTER LOOKS
- A character's name sets its look: the Black Cat is always black. Players tell cats apart by a coloured tummy.
- Don't put colour where it could read as a mouth. The cat uses a black chin with a tummy patch lower down.
- Abilities should look like the character: NINE LIVES is the cat's tail curled over his head.
